What counts as large text

WCAG treats text as large from 24px (18pt) at regular weight, or from 18.66px (14pt) in bold. Everything smaller is body text and needs 4.5:1 for level AA.

The ratio is rounded down, so a pair shown as 4.50:1 really does meet 4.5:1.
